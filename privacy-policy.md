# Privacy Policy — Skyburst

**Last updated:** 9 October 2026
**Applies to:** Skyburst for Android (`com.kairiv.skyburst`)
**Published by:** Kairiv Games — eliutdev@gmail.com

---

## The short version

Skyburst is a single-player arcade game. It does not ask you to make an account,
and it does not know who you are.

Almost everything the game stores — your progress, your stardust, your purchases,
your settings — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Three things do leave your device:

1. **If you submit a score to the leaderboard**, the name you chose, your score,
   and which stage you reached are sent to our leaderboard provider.
2. **Ads.** Skyburst shows optional rewarded video ads, supplied by Google
   AdMob. You never have to watch one; every ad in the game is something you
   choose to start in exchange for a reward. Google collects data to serve those
   ads, and in some regions we ask your permission first.
3. **Anonymous gameplay statistics**, so we can see how the game is actually
   being played and balance it — which stage runs tend to end on, which upgrades
   people pick, how often a feature gets used — and whether the ads we run to
   find new players are bringing people who enjoy the game. It is counted, never
   watched: there is no recording and no session replay. Where the law requires
   your permission, this is switched off until you give it, and off for good if
   you decline.

That is the whole of it. Nothing is sold to anyone, there is no crash reporting,
and nothing we collect is used to build a profile of you or to target you with
advertising.

## What is stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It lives in the app's private storage and
is removed when you uninstall the game.

- Game progress: your current stage, best score, level and experience.
- Your stardust balances and everything you have bought or equipped.
- Skill points and blaster upgrades.
- Settings: volume, language, graphics mode, and whether you have seen the
  tutorial.
- Custom stages you have created.
- The display name you chose for the leaderboard, and a random identifier the
  game generates for itself (see below).

We cannot see any of it.

## What is sent to the leaderboard

Only when you submit a score. If you never enter a name, nothing is ever sent.

Our leaderboard provider is **CheddaBoards** (`api.cheddaboards.com`). Each
submission contains:

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Display name | The name you typed. You choose it, and it does not have to be your real name — please do not use one. |
| Score | Your score. |
| Stage and chain length | Which stage you reached and how long your chain was. |
| A random player identifier | Explained below. |

**About that identifier.** So that the leaderboard can show one row per player
rather than one per attempt, the game generates a random string the first time
it runs and stores it on your device. It is made from the current time and two
random numbers. It is **not** your advertising ID, your device ID, your phone
number, or anything issued by your phone or by Google — it is meaningless
outside this game, it is not shared with anyone other than the leaderboard
provider, and uninstalling the game destroys it.

We do not collect your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos,
or any other personal information, because the game never asks for them.

## Advertising

Skyburst shows **rewarded ads only**. There are no banners, no pop-ups, and no
ads between stages. An ad only ever plays because you pressed a button offering
you something in return — a retry, extra stardust, or a free level.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve them, Google may collect and
process information including your device's advertising ID, device and network
information, and interactions with the ads. That processing is governed by
Google's own policies, not ours:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choice, where the law gives you one.** If you are in the European
Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open
Skyburst you will be shown a consent form asking whether Google may use your
data for personalised advertising. Nothing is requested from Google's ad
services until you answer.

You can change your answer at any time: **Settings → Privacy Options**.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's own
settings, under *Settings → Google → Ads*.

## Gameplay statistics

Skyburst is made by one person, and the only way to tell whether a stage is too
hard, an upgrade is worth taking, or a feature is ever found is to count what
happens. That counting is done with **Google Analytics for Firebase**.

**What is counted.** Only whole events, with numbers attached:

- a run started and ended — which mode, which stage it reached, roughly what it
  scored, the longest chain it made;
- which upgrade cards were chosen, and at which stage;
- whether an optional retry was offered, taken or declined;
- a milestone being reached for the first time.

Alongside those, Google records standard technical information: your device
model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate country worked
out from your IP address. Google does not give us your IP address.

**Measuring our own ads.** We advertise Skyburst on Google Ads. To learn which
of those ads bring players who keep playing — rather than only counting
installs — the statistics include your device's **advertising ID**, which lets
Google match what happens in the game (for example, a rewarded ad being watched)
to the ad that led to the install. This is measurement only, it is covered by the
same permission as the rest of the statistics, and you can reset or delete your
advertising ID at any time in Android's settings.

**What is deliberately switched off.** These are turned off in the app itself,
not merely left unticked in a dashboard:

- your Android device ID is not collected;
- nothing collected here is used for ad personalisation, or to build an audience
  to target you with ads.

**What is never sent.** Your leaderboard name, your player identifier, the names
of stages you create, the contents of anything you write, and any free text at
all. Every value sent is either a number or one word chosen from a fixed list
written into the game.

**Your choice.** Where you are asked for consent, the counting stays off until
you agree, and stops if you withdraw — the same **Settings → Privacy Options**
form that controls the ads controls this too, and withdrawing takes effect
immediately, not the next time you open the game.

**How long.** These statistics are kept for **two months** and then deleted.

## Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK)

- **Personalised advertising** — your consent, given through the form described
  above, which you may withdraw at any time.
- **Non-personalised advertising and basic ad delivery** — our legitimate
  interest in funding a free game, to the extent permitted where you are.
- **Leaderboard submission** — your consent, given by choosing to submit a
  score. You are never required to.
- **Gameplay statistics** — your consent, given through the same form as the
  advertising choice above and withdrawable in the same place. Until it is
  given, nothing is counted.

## Children

Skyburst is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and we do
not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child
has submitted personal information — for example, using a real name as their
leaderboard name — contact us at the address above and we will remove it.

---

## How long things are kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Gameplay statistics are kept for two months and then deleted.

Leaderboard entries are kept by our provider for as long as the leaderboard
exists. Because the name you submit is one you chose and the identifier attached
to it is random, an entry does not identify you to us.

**If you want a leaderboard entry removed, email us and we will have it
removed.** Changing your name in Settings also re-submits your best score under
the new name, and the board shows only the newest entry for a player, so the old
one stops being displayed.

## Who your data is shared with

Only the two service providers that make those features work:

- **Google AdMob** — advertising. See the links above.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** — the gameplay statistics described above.
  Governed by the same Google policies linked in the advertising section.
- **CheddaBoards** — leaderboard hosting.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete
or export your personal data, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent.
For anything held on your device, uninstalling the app exercises all of these at
once. For a leaderboard entry, or for anything else, contact us at the address
at the top of this policy and we will respond.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
